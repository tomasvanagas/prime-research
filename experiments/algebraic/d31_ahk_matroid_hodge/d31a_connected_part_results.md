# D31.a — Connected-part-only AHK deviation

Sub-experiment of D31 (CLOSED in S149, see
`d31_ahk_matroid_hodge_results.md` §3.5 for the full discussion).

**Pointer:** all results are reported in
`d31_ahk_matroid_hodge_results.md` §3.5 and §5.

**Numerical data:** `d31a_connected_part_data.json`.

**Outcome:** the configuration-model deviation persists on the
connected-part `M_conn^N` after stripping the Bertrand
`(t − 1)^{π(N) − π(N/2)}` direct-summand factor. At `N = 20` with
50 controls: `z(δ_1) = +3.91σ`, `z(δ_2) = +3.46σ`, `z(δ_3) = +2.63σ`,
and `z(|w_k|) = +2.41σ to +2.88σ` for `k = 0..3`. About half of the
raw `M_P^N` deviation traces to the Bertrand factor; the other half
is residual structure of `M_conn^N` not yet identified analytically.

**Falsification:** would have triggered (E) closure if conn-only
deviation collapsed below `±2σ`. Did not — closes mode I.
